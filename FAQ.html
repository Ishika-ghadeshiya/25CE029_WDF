Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is StudentHub?
StudentHub is an online portal where students can access academic information such as timetables, attendance, assignments, and notices.
2. Who can use StudentHub?
Students and faculty members of the college can use the portal.
3. How do I log in?
Click on the Login button and enter your registered username and password.
4. Can I check my attendance?
Yes, students can view their attendance after logging into the portal.
